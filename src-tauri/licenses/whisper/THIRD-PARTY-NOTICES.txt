Whisper model weights and whisper.cpp runtime
============================================

Voxloom can explicitly download converted OpenAI Whisper model weights from
https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp. Model weights are not bundled
with the application. The OpenAI model weights and whisper.cpp runtime are
licensed under MIT. Their upstream notices follow.
