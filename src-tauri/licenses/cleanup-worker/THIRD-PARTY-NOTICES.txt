Third-party notices: voxloom-cleanup-worker
(Contents/MacOS/voxloom-cleanup-worker, BUS-16 PR 3c-2b)

The cleanup worker statically links the components below. Each notice is
reproduced as its licence requires. Where a component is offered under a
choice of licences (e.g. "MIT OR Apache-2.0"), Voxloom uses it under MIT.
Covers src-tauri/worker/Cargo.lock as of llama-cpp-2 =0.1.160 (vendored
llama.cpp d81235049384534c167caea52b85a694f6103d14); update it when the
worker's dependencies change.

Not linked: llama.cpp's common/ library and its vendor/ code (cpp-httplib,
nlohmann/json, minja, stb, miniaudio). They are compiled by llama-cpp-sys-2
but no symbol from them is in the binary (checked with nm, 3c-2b).
Also not linked: llama.cpp's DRY sampler (src/llama-sampler.cpp, ported
from koboldcpp, with a public-domain Z-algorithm); none of its strings are
in the binary. Not compiled for the shipped targets: the SVE exp routine
from OpenVINO (ggml-cpu vec.h, SVE builds only) and the other ggml backends.
