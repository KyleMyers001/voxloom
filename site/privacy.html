On-device

Privacy

Voxloom transcribes speech on your Mac. Here is exactly what touches the network and what stays local.

TRANSCRIPTION

Speech-to-text runs locally

Audio is transcribed on your Mac with whisper.cpp. Voxloom has no cloud speech-to-text path.

NETWORK

The one download: models

Whisper model files come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp), and only after you choose to download them. There are no silent downloads.

STORAGE

What's saved, and where

  • History: transcript text, status, duration, model, timestamp and delivery outcome, saved in history.json in the app's data folder on your Mac.
  • Transcript retention setting: Off (no transcripts kept), 30 days, or until deleted.
  • Word dictionary: dictionary.json. Settings: settings.json. Both are stored locally.
  • Recorded audio isn't saved for later. Saved audio retention is not implemented.
CLIPBOARD

Copy fallback

If inserting text isn't safe and clipboard fallback is enabled, the text is copied to your clipboard. Voxloom never types into password fields and never presses Send.
